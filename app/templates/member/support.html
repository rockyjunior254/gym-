Frequently Asked Questions

How do payments work at Strakly?
Strakly operates strictly on a physical cash basis. Membership plans, merchandise reservations, and personal training top-ups are paid directly to reception staff in cash.
How is attendance recorded?
Attendance is strictly recorded manually by our front-desk receptionists upon your entry to the facility. Your dashboard and calendar update automatically following check-in.
How do I freeze my membership?
Navigate to the "My Membership" module and click "Request Plan Change". Select "Membership Freeze", enter your travel or medical dates, and submit for front-desk approval.
