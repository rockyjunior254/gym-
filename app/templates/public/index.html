HAVE QUESTIONS?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How does cash payment work?

All membership dues are paid in physical cash directly at the gym reception desk. Staff record the cash transaction in the system, which generates a unique verifiable receipt (e.g. RCP-2026-XXXX) available for viewing and printing in your Member Portal.

How is daily attendance recorded?

Attendance is recorded manually by authorised front-desk staff. When you enter, the desk attendant searches your name or member ID, confirms your active subscription status, and records your check-in. Strictly no QR code scanning or biometrics are used.

What is the difference between a coached and self-guided member?

Both use the same underlying Member account! Self-guided members manage their own workouts and logs. Coached members have an authorised trainer assigned by administration, unlocking custom workout plan builders, scheduled coaching sessions, and trainer feedback.

Can I participate in the Community Hub?

Yes! The Community Hub is shared across all members, trainers, and administrators. You can share training achievements, join special-interest groups, participate in discussions, and connect with fellow gym athletes.
